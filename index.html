About Dan Fountain & Co

The builder you deal with is the builder on your site

Dan Fountain & Co is an award winning building company based in Wānaka. Dan and the crew build architectural homes, renovations and high-end interiors across the Upper Clutha, and every job gets the same standard: square, solid and finished properly.

As Registered Master Builders, the work is backed by the Master Build Guarantee. You deal directly with Dan from pricing to handover, so decisions get made fast and nothing is lost in translation.
